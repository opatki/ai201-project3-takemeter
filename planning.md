## Labels

### Data-Driven Analysis:
The user brings receipts. They use advanced stats (TS%, RAPTOR), salary cap math, or specific film breakdowns to make a point.

### Reactionary / Hot Take:
Pure emotion, usually right after a game. Sweeping declarations based on small sample sizes (e.g., "Player X is washed," "This team will never win a ring").

### Narrative Debate:
Focuses on legacies, MVP races, rankings, or hypotheticals rather than on-court X's and O's (e.g., "Does winning a ring this year put Jokic in the top 10 all-time?").

### News & Aggregation:
Factual reporting of trades, Woj/Shams tweets, injury updates, or direct quotes from player interviews.
